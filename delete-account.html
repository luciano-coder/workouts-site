Delete your account

The free app has no account: everything you log stays on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it.

If you use the Coach, you have an account with us (Stellar Spectra Ltd). Deleting it removes your account, workout summaries, coach settings and state, coaching, and the records of your coach runs and their cost. Encrypted backups are kept for 30 days, so they're gone from those within 30 days. Nothing is kept after that. The workouts on your phone stay there.

In the app

  • Open Settings, then Coach account.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email apps@stellarspectra.co.uk from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, asking us to delete your Workouts account. We'll delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.
